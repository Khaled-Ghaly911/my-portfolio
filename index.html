Clinicova: multi-tenant clinic management

  • NestJS
  • TypeScript
  • MySQL
  • Prisma
  • Redis
  • BullMQ
  • Socket.IO

A platform where multiple clinics manage patients, appointments, doctors, staff, clinical workflows, billing and notifications, each on an isolated tenant database.

  • Database-per-tenant architecture

    A Master Database holds tenant metadata and connection details. AsyncLocalStorage carries tenant context through each request; middleware and a central TenantService resolve the tenant.

  • Tenant connection reuse

    Prisma Client instances are cached per tenant with an LRU cache, so connections are reused instead of recreated.

  • Automated tenant provisioning

    A BullMQ and Redis workflow creates the master record, provisions the database, runs migrations and seeds data. Status tracking lets the mobile app follow onboarding.

  • Access and real-time

    JWT with refresh tokens, role-based permissions for owners, doctors and receptionists, and Socket.IO alerts between doctors and staff.

Business workflows
  • Patients, appointment scheduling, doctor and staff management.
  • Medical records, prescriptions, billing and notifications.
